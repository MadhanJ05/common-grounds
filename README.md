# Common Grounds

**Grab a coffee to meet someone.**

Pay for your coffee with PayPal and you join that café's circle. For the next 7 days you can meet other people who paid there. Pay any other way and you just get coffee.

- **One-to-one, words first.** Each person has a reusable card: a nickname and three short prompts, with no photo and no age shown.
- **A quiet hello.** A chat opens only when both people say hi.
- **Mutual age filter.** You only see someone if each of you falls inside the other's age range. A card shows *Unverified* when the person's age isn't verified.
- **Coffee's on me.** When a chat turns into a plan ("coffee tmrw after my 10am?"), Claude works out the day, time and café, then sets up a PayPal hold for the other person's coffee. It's captured when they redeem it and voided if they decline or don't show.
- **Where to next.** A map of nearby cafés that accept PayPal, each with its own circle to join.
